Street sweeping - 19th St by Dolores Park

San Francisco - both sides of every block.
Each side of the street is its own line, drawn on that side of the curb and colored by sweep day. Red pulsing = being swept now. Tap a line for the schedule; tap a block name below to find it on the map.
Source: DataSF Street Sweeping Schedule (yhqp-riqs), snapshot pulled Sep 24, 2026 (dataset last updated May 14, 2026). "1st, 3rd, 5th" means that occurrence of the weekday in the month. Daytime sweeping is not enforced on some city holidays - see the SFMTA holiday schedule. Posted signs always win - check the sign before you park. 19th St has no Dolores-Church block (the park sits there), so that gap is expected.
